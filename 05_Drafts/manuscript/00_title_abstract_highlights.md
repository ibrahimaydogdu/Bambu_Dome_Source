## Abstract
This study investigates single-objective discrete sizing optimization and cradle-to-gate embodied carbon dynamics for 40-m<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:48 --> single-layer bamboo spatial domes under ISO 22156:2021<!-- [REF: ISO 22156:2021] --> and IStructE (2025)<!-- [REF: Trujillo et al. 2025] --> Allowable Stress Design provisions. Three dome topologies (Geodesic, Kiewitt, and Ribbed) are optimized across a 24-section<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:49 --> discrete *Guadua angustifolia* catalog using Artificial Bee Colony (ABC), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), and Dandelion Optimizer (DO). A computational campaign of 270 independent runs<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:57 --> across 30 random seeds produced 100% feasible designs verified against SAP2000 (relative error $\le 1.3 \times 10^{-13}$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:59 -->). ABC demonstrated superior convergence consistency and statistical dominance (Holm-adjusted Mann-Whitney $p \le 5.53 \times 10^{-4}$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:58 -->, $A_{12} \ge 0.758$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:58 -->), obtaining minimum member weights of 5.80 kN<!-- T1 -->, 41.06 kN<!-- T1 -->, and 66.35 kN<!-- T1 -->.

While bamboo culm members achieve 32.3% to 49.1%<!-- T6 --> weight savings compared to identical-geometry structural steel pipe counterparts, life cycle assessment reveals the Decarbonization Paradox: steel connector plates, bolts, and cementitious grout infill account for 74.1% to 82.1%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:65 --> of total cradle-to-gate embodied carbon in the baseline scenario. Sensitivity analysis indicates this dominance is conditional, decreasing to 45.3%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:65 --> under intercontinental transport and low-carbon steel. Due to grout infill, total assembled bamboo system weight is more than double that of the steel dome in the ribbed configuration (277.2 kN vs 135.0 kN<!-- T5 -->). Material procurement cost ratios range from 0.61 to 1.39<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:73 --> across international price models, with non-shrink grout representing 41.6% to 73.4%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:73 --> of bamboo material expenditures. These findings underscore that true decarbonization of bio-structural spatial grids requires low-carbon joint innovation rather than member sizing alone.
