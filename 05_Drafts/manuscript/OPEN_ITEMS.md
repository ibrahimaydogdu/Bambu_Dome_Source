# Open items

## Decisions taken by the authors (2026-10-13)

1. **Emission factors.** The factors for connection steel (2.38), steel tube (2.51) and grout (0.18 kg CO₂e/kg) come from ICE Database Educational V5.0, and so does the production upper bound of the culm factor EC_b (softwood without carbon storage, 0.2626 kg CO₂e/kg, `EC_BAMBOO_METHOD.md`). The authors decided to use these values. The "Please Read First" document of the database states that it may not be used "in research or any carbon calculations, other than for the purposes of teaching or learning the subject of embodied carbon". This licence note is kept here for the submission stage; it is not a blocker for the draft.
2. **Title.** The working title was changed by the lead author's authority to a plain descriptive title.
3. **Price scenarios.** The Indonesian scenario is the main case; the Indian, Türkiye and Colombian scenarios are sensitivity cases.
4. **Population sizes.** The sizes used (20, 40, 40) are justified by their ratio to the number of design variables and by Taymuş et al. (2025), who set the memory size equal to the number of design variables. Further papers of the authors can be added to this justification if their PDFs are placed in `Papers/`.
