# 5. Conclusions

This study investigated the single-objective structural sizing optimization and cradle-to-gate embodied carbon dynamics of three 40.0 m<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:48 --> single-layer bamboo spatial domes (Ribbed, Kiewitt, and Geodesic) under codified ISO 22156:2021 / IStructE (2025)<!-- [REF: Trujillo et al. 2025] --> ASD provisions and ASCE 7-22 load combinations. Based on 270 independent optimization runs and life cycle accounting, the primary findings are:

1. **Structural Sizing Optimization and Algorithmic Performance:** Across all 270 campaign executions, 100% of runs converged to strictly feasible designs ($P = 0$)<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:57 --> verified against SAP2000 (relative error $\le 1.3 \times 10^{-13}$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:59 -->). The Artificial Bee Colony (ABC) algorithm attained global minimum structural weights of 5.800 kN<!-- T1 --> (`geodesic16`), 41.058 kN<!-- T1 --> (`kiewitt32`), and 66.352 kN<!-- T1 --> (`ribbed40`). Non-parametric Friedman tests ($\chi^2_F = 38.26 - 49.16$, $p < 0.001$<!-- STATISTICAL_TEST_RESULTS.md -->) and Holm-adjusted Mann-Whitney U tests ($p_{\text{Holm}} \le 5.53 \times 10^{-4}$, $A_{12} \ge 0.758$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:58 -->) demonstrated the statistical superiority of ABC over Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and the Dandelion Optimizer (DO).
2. **The Decarbonization Paradox:** In spatial truss assemblies, hybrid connection hardware (steel plates, bolts, and cementitious grout) dominates cradle-to-gate embodied emissions, representing 74.1% to 82.1%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:65 --> of total structural carbon in the baseline scenario (only 17.9% to 25.9%<!-- T7 --> stems from raw bamboo culms). Sensitivity analysis confirms this relationship is conditional: under intercontinental sea shipping ($EC_b = 1.23\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:64 -->) and low-carbon steel plate ($EC_s = 1.60\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$<!-- T8 -->), the joint carbon fraction in the Kiewitt dome declines to 45.3%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:65 -->. Whole-system carbon accounting is mandatory to avoid greenwashing in bio-structural engineering.
3. **Member versus Total System Weight Disparity:** While bamboo culm members are 32.3% to 49.1% lighter than equivalent structural steel pipe members<!-- T6 -->, total assembled system mass reverses this trend. In the 40 m ribbed configuration, the bamboo dome system weighs 277.2 kN<!-- T5 --> (including 186.6 kN<!-- [DATA NEEDED: grout weight] --> of cementitious grout) compared to 129.0 kN<!-- T5 --> member weight and 135.0 kN<!-- T5 --> total weight for the benchmark steel dome, more than doubling total dead weight.
4. **Economic Material Parity:** Material procurement cost ratios (Bamboo/Steel) range from 0.61–0.87 in `geodesic16`, 0.87–1.39 in `kiewitt32`, and 0.98–1.34 in `ribbed40` across four regional pricing models (Indonesia, India, Turkiye, Colombia)<!-- T9 -->. Non-shrink cementitious grout represents 41.6% to 73.4%<!-- 00_System/DOCS_UPDATE_PROMPT.md:73 --> of total bamboo structure material expenditure.
5. **Physical Detailing Limitations:** In the dense `ribbed40` mesh, 12 members (0.82%, length 0.76 m<!-- T10 -->) carry forces requiring 3 through-bolts ($N \le 31.25\text{ kN}$<!-- T10 -->), which cannot physically fit codified longitudinal bolt spacing rules ($\ge 1.05\text{ m}$<!-- 04_Results/PAPER_METHODS_NOTES.md:13 --> required). This identifies an essential detailing boundary for bio-based spatial grid topologies.

**Future Work:** Research should focus on full-scale experimental testing of low-carbon bio-composite connectors (e.g., bio-based resins, engineered timber gussets), cradle-to-grave life cycle scenarios including end-of-life biogenic carbon recovery, and non-linear dynamic wind-structure interaction modeling.
