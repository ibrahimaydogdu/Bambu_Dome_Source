## Figures

- **Figure 1 (F1):** Optimum cross-sectional profile distribution across sizing groups for 40 m bamboo spatial domes: (a) Geodesic (`geodesic16`), (b) Kiewitt (`kiewitt32`), and (c) Ribbed (`ribbed40`). Colors denote outer diameter ($D$) and wall thickness ($t$) indices from the 24-section *Guadua angustifolia* catalog.
- **Figure 2 (F2):** Convergence profiles of penalized structural weight [kN] versus Number of Function Evaluations (NFE) across 30 independent runs (seeds 1001–1030) for ABC, ACO, and DO: (a) `geodesic16` (23k NFEs), (b) `kiewitt32` (22k NFEs), and (c) `ribbed40` (43k NFEs). - **Figure 3 (F3):** Statistical distribution of optimum structural weight [kN] over 30 independent seeds for the three metaheuristic algorithms: (a) `geodesic16`, (b) `kiewitt32`, and (c) `ribbed40`. - **Figure 5a (F5_weight):** Comparative structural weight breakdown between optimum bamboo domes and conventional structural steel spatial grids: member weight [kN] versus total assembled system dead weight [kN] incorporating steel gussets, hubs, and cementitious grout infill.
- **Figure 5b (F5_co2):** Cradle-to-gate (A1–A3) embodied carbon breakdown [t CO$_2$e] of optimum bamboo domes by constituent component: raw bamboo culms, metallic connection hardware (gusset plates, hubs, bolts), and non-shrink cementitious grout infill.
